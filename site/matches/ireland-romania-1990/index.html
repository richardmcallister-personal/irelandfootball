World Cup 1990, last 16

Ireland 0–0 Romania

Ireland won 5–4 on penalties, after extra time.
Monday 25 June 1990, 17:00 local time. Stadio Luigi Ferraris, Genoa. Crowd 31818. Referee José Roberto Wright (Brazil). Manager Jack Charlton.

This was Ireland’s first World Cup. Beating Romania put them into the quarter-finals at the first attempt, and they got there without winning a game in 90 minutes: three group draws, two goals, then this 0–0. The game extended their unbeaten run to 17 matches, then a record.

Romania kicked first. FIFA’s full shoot-out is on FIFA+, which now opens on DAZN.

Daniel Timofte

Romania’s midfielder came on after 98 minutes, in place of Ioan Sabău, and took their fifth kick with the shoot-out level at 4–4. Packie Bonner saved it, the only kick of the ten that did not go in.

David O’Leary

The Arsenal defender did not get on the pitch in the group games. He replaced Steve Staunton after 94 minutes and, by his own account, took number five because it was the last number left. His kick sent Ireland through. As RTÉ’s George Hamilton put it, “The nation holds its breath.”
