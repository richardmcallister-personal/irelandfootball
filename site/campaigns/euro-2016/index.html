Euro 2016

Long beat Germany, Brady beat Italy

Ireland finished third in Group D behind Germany and Poland. Shane Long’s goal beat Germany, the world champions, 1–0 in Dublin on 8 October 2015. In the play-off they drew 1–1 with Bosnia and Herzegovina in Zenica and won 2–0 in Dublin, Jonathan Walters scoring both.

In France, Wes Hoolahan scored in a 1–1 draw with Sweden, Belgium won 3–0, and Robbie Brady’s goal four minutes from time beat Italy 1–0 to take Ireland through as one of the best third-placed teams. Brady scored again in the second minute against France in Lyon, but Antoine Griezmann scored twice and France won 2–1.
