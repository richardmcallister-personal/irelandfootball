Italia ’90

Ireland’s first World Cup: five games, one defeat, a quarter-final in Rome

Ireland qualified second in Group 6, a point behind Spain, with five wins in eight games and two goals conceded. Their only defeat was in Seville.

In Italy they drew all three group games, with England, Egypt and the Netherlands. Ireland and the Netherlands finished level on everything, and lots were drawn for second place. Ireland went to Genoa and beat Romania on penalties. On 30 June they lost 1–0 to Italy in Rome; Salvatore Schillaci scored.
