Euro ’88

Ireland’s first finals, decided in a game they did not play

Ireland finished their qualifying group on 14 October 1987, top on 11 points, and could do nothing more. Bulgaria had a game left, at home to Scotland in Sofia, and a draw would have put Bulgaria through on goal difference.

On 11 November 1987 Scotland, who could no longer qualify, won 1–0. Gary Mackay, a substitute, scored four minutes from time. Seven months later, in Ireland’s first game at a major finals, Ray Houghton scored after six minutes to beat England. Jack Charlton was the manager.
