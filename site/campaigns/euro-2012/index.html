Euro 2012

Second in the group, 4–0 in Tallinn, three defeats in Poland

Ireland finished second in Group B, two points behind Russia, and met Estonia in a play-off. They won 4–0 in Tallinn, Robbie Keane scoring twice, and drew 1–1 in Dublin.

At the finals they lost all three games: 3–1 to Croatia in Poznań, 4–0 to Spain in Gdańsk and 2–0 to Italy in Poznań. Sean St Ledger scored Ireland’s only goal.
