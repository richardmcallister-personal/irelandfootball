World Cup 2002

Unbeaten in qualifying, a stoppage-time goal against Germany, out on penalties to Spain

Ireland went through qualifying unbeaten and finished level with Portugal on 24 points, second on goal difference. Jason McAteer’s goal beat the Netherlands 1–0 in Dublin on 1 September 2001 and kept the Dutch out. A play-off with Iran followed: 2–0 in Dublin, 1–0 to Iran in Tehran.

Before the finals Roy Keane left the squad and was not replaced. In Japan, Matt Holland scored in a 1–1 draw with Cameroon, Robbie Keane equalised against Germany in stoppage time, and a 3–0 win over Saudi Arabia put Ireland second in Group E. Against Spain in Suwon, Keane’s penalty in the 90th minute made it 1–1. Spain won the shoot-out 3–2.
