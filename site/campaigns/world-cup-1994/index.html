USA ’94

Through on goals scored, then Houghton’s goal beat Italy

Ireland finished Group 3 level with Denmark on 18 points and the same goal difference, and went through on goals scored, 19 to 15. Spain won the group. Ireland’s last game was in Belfast on 17 November 1993: Northern Ireland led through Jimmy Quinn, and Alan McLoughlin equalised soon after.

At the finals Ray Houghton scored the only goal against Italy at Giants Stadium. Mexico won 2–1 in Orlando, and a goalless draw with Norway left all four teams in Group E on four points. Ireland finished second; Norway went out on goals scored. The Netherlands won the last-16 game 2–0 in Orlando.
