{{Short description|Football tournament group stage}}
'''Group E of [[UEFA Euro 2016]]''' contained [[Belgium national football team|Belgium]], [[Italy national football team|Italy]], [[Republic of Ireland national football team|Republic of Ireland]] and [[Sweden men's national football team|Sweden]]. Italy was the only former European champion in this group, having won in [[Euro 1968|1968]]. Matches were played from 13 to 22 June 2016.

In the [[UEFA Euro 2016 knockout stage#Round of 16|round of 16]],
*The winner of Group E, Italy, advanced to play the runner-up of [[UEFA Euro 2016 Group D|Group D]], [[Spain national football team|Spain]].
*The runner-up of Group E, Belgium, advanced to play the winner of [[UEFA Euro 2016 Group F|Group F]], [[Hungary national football team|Hungary]].
*The third-placed team of Group E, Republic of Ireland, advanced as one of the [[UEFA Euro 2016#Ranking of third-placed teams|four best third-placed teams]] to play the winner of [[UEFA Euro 2016 Group A|Group A]], [[France national football team|France]].
