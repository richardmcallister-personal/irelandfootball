The '''1990 FIFA World Cup qualification UEFA Group 6''' was a [[UEFA]] qualifying group for the [[1990 FIFA World Cup]]. The group consisted of [[Hungary national football team|Hungary]], [[Malta national football team|Malta]], [[Northern Ireland national football team|Northern Ireland]], [[Spain national football team|Spain]] and the [[Republic of Ireland national football team|Republic of Ireland]].

The group was won by Spain, who qualified for the 1990 FIFA World Cup. The Republic of Ireland also qualified as runners-up for their first ever World Cup Finals.<ref>[https://italia1990.com/uefa/group-6/ 1990 Qualification – UEFA Group 6], Italia1990.com</ref>
