[[File:Euro 2012 qualifying play-offs draw (2).jpg|thumb|[[Gianni Infantino]] (left) and [[Zbigniew Boniek]] during a draw for the play-offs|300 px]]
The '''UEFA Euro 2012 qualifying play-off''' ties were played over [[two-legged tie|two legs]], with the first legs on 11 November and the second legs on 15 November 2011.<ref>{{cite web|title=UEFA Euro 2012 qualifying play-offs|url=https://www.uefa.com/uefaeuro/history/seasons/2012/playoffs/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210422203446/https://www.uefa.com/uefaeuro/history/seasons/2012/playoffs/|url-status=dead|archive-date=April 22, 2021|work=[[UEFA]]|publisher=Union of European Football Associations|access-date=5 October 2010}}</ref> The four winners are found according to the standard rules for the knockout phase in European competitions, and the winners qualified for the [[UEFA Euro 2012|Euro 2012]] tournament.<ref name="regulations">{{cite web |url=https://www.uefa.com/MultimediaFiles/Download/competitions/euro/91/87/57/918757_download.pdf |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100215034039/http://www.uefa.com/multimediafiles/download/competitions/euro/91/87/57/918757_download.pdf |url-status=dead |archive-date=February 15, 2010 |title=Regulations of the UEFA European Football Championship 2010–12 |date=September 2009 |publisher=UEFA |pages=6–7 |access-date=3 September 2010}}</ref> Qualifying play-offs was a second stage (round) of [[UEFA Euro 2012 qualifying]].

==Ranking of second-placed teams==
The highest ranked second placed team from the groups qualified automatically for the tournament, while the remainder entered the playoffs. As six groups contained six teams and three with five, matches against the sixth-placed team in each group were discarded in this ranking. As a result, a total of eight matches played by each team count toward the purpose of the second-placed ranking table.

==Seedings==
[[File:Zbigniew Boniek 2011 (1).jpg|thumb|[[Zbigniew Boniek]] during the draw]]
The draw for the play-offs was held on 13 October 2011 in [[Kraków]], Poland, to determine the four pairings as well as the order of the home and away ties.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.uefa.com/uefaeuro/history/news/0254-0d7d37b52520-0429e8098110-1000--draw-for-the-uefa-euro-2012-play-offs/ |title=Draw for the UEFA EURO 2012 play-offs |date=13 October 2011 |publisher=UEFA}}</ref>

After [[2010 FIFA World Cup qualification – UEFA second round#Seeding controversy|the controversy caused by the UEFA play-offs during 2010 FIFA World Cup qualifying]] – which was originally to be an unseeded draw, but was later altered by FIFA to a seeded one – UEFA stated from the outset that the draw for the play-offs would be seeded.<ref name="regulations"/> The four runners-up with the best positions in the [[UEFA coefficient#Men's national team coefficient|UEFA team coefficient]] ranking system were therefore seeded.

Each nation's coefficient was generated by calculating:<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.uefa.com/MultimediaFiles/Download/competitions/General/01/69/56/52/1695652_DOWNLOAD.pdf |title=National Team Coefficient Ranking |date=12 October 2011 |publisher=UEFA}}</ref>
*40% of the average ranking points per game earned in the [[UEFA Euro 2012 qualifying#Qualifying group stage|UEFA Euro 2012 qualifying group stage]].
*40% of the average ranking points per game earned in the [[2010 FIFA World Cup qualification (UEFA)|2010 FIFA World Cup qualifying stage]] and [[2010 FIFA World Cup|final tournament]].
*20% of the average ranking points per game earned in the [[UEFA Euro 2008 qualifying|UEFA Euro 2008 qualifying stage]] and [[UEFA Euro 2008|final tournament]].
