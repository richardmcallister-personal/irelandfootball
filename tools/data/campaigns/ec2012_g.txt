{{Short description|Football tournament group stage}}
'''Group C of [[UEFA Euro 2012]]''' began on 10 June 2012 and ended on 18 June 2012. 
The pool consisted of defending (and eventual) champion [[Spain national football team|Spain]], [[Italy national football team|Italy]], [[Republic of Ireland national football team|Republic of Ireland]] and [[Croatia national football team|Croatia]]. The group was jokingly dubbed the "group of debt" by multiple media outlets, in reference to the [[European sovereign debt crisis]] facing some of its members.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://bleacherreport.com/articles/1216488-euro-2012-championships-group-cs-italy-spain-and-crotia-provide-spark|title=Euro 2012: Group C's Italy, Spain and Croatia Provide Spark|first=Mike|last=Raffone|website=Bleacher Report}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.goal.com/en/news/2898/euro-2012/2012/06/03/3144067/a-welcome-distraction-euro-2012-a-timely-respite-for|title=A welcome distraction: Euro 2012 a timely respite for depressed & debt-ridden Spain|website=Goal.com}}</ref> Spain and Italy progressed to the quarter-finals, while Croatia and Republic of Ireland were eliminated from the tournament. Republic of Ireland equalled the worst performance by a team in the group stage of the [[UEFA European Championship|European Championships]], finishing with no points and a goal difference of −8.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Fitzmaurice |first1=Aidan |title=Tired limbs, bored players and a hotel next to a lap-dancing club: Ireland's Euro 2012 campaign was doomed from the start |url=https://www.independent.ie/sport/when-a-nation-held-its-breath/tired-limbs-bored-players-and-a-hotel-next-to-a-lap-dancing-club-irelands-euro-2012-campaign-was-doomed-from-the-start-39279295.html |access-date=27 October 2020 |work=Irish Independent |date=12 June 2020}}</ref> Both Spain and Italy made it through the quarter-finals and semi-finals to reach the final for a second meeting in the tournament.

In their final match, the Republic of Ireland wore black armbands to commemorate the 18th anniversary of the [[Loughinisland massacre]]. This was criticised by some unionists and members of the [[Ulster Volunteer Force|UVF]].<ref name="beltel2012">[http://www.belfasttelegraph.co.uk/news/local-national/northern-ireland/irelands-euro-2012-armband-match-tribute-to-uvf-massacre-victims-slammed-16172414.html "Ireland's Euro 2012 armband match tribute to UVF massacre victims slammed". ''Belfast Telegraph''. Brian Rowan. 14 June 2012] Retrieved 19 June 2012</ref> However, the victims' families fully supported the gesture.<ref name="beltel2012"/>

Spain, Italy, and Croatia were later drawn in [[UEFA Euro 2024 Group B|Group B of the UEFA Euro 2024]]; coincidentally, Croatia would be eliminated in the group while Spain would also go on to win the tournament as well; Republic of Ireland did not qualify for this Euro.
