The [[Republic of Ireland national football team|Republic of Ireland]] have appeared in the Men's [[FIFA World Cup]] in [[1990 FIFA World Cup|1990]], [[1994 FIFA World Cup|1994]], and [[2002 FIFA World Cup|2002]]. They have always advanced from the group stage but have never advanced beyond the quarter-finals.

Their first appearance was in Italy at the 1990 Men's FIFA World Cup.<ref>{{cite web|url= https://italia1990.com/uefa/group-6/malta-rep-of-ireland/|title=Accomplished Irish performance in which two Aldridge goals see them through to the World Cup|date=15 November 1989|work=Italia1990.com|accessdate=19 November 2025}}</ref>
